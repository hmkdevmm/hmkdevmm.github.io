Deleting Your Data

Your pictures, your drafts and the fonts you added are held on your phone and nowhere else, so uninstalling the app deletes them. Nothing is kept for me to delete.

Two things are held away from your phone, and both can be removed:

  • Crash reports and advertising records. These carry no name, email or account, only a device identifier. You can reset or delete that identifier at any time from Settings › Privacy › Ads on your phone, which unlinks the records from your device.

If you would like anything deleted and would rather I did it, email hmkdevjp@hotmail.com with the name of the app and I will deal with it within 30 days.
