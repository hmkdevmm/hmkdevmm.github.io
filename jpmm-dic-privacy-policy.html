NichiMM — Privacy Policy

Developer: HMK DEV MM · Effective date: 18 November 2026 · Also available in Myanmar and Japanese inside the App (Menu → Privacy).

1. Summary

NichiMM (the “App”) is made by HMK DEV MM (“we”, “us”). You can use the App without an account, and most of your learning data stays on your device. Some features need Google services (Firebase) to work: the optional sign-in and cloud backup, the AI features, and rewarded ads that you choose to watch. We do not sell your data.

2. Data stored on your device

  • Favourites, history, your own words, flashcard and quiz progress, lessons, AI corrections and saved AI explanations, your profile name and picture choice, and your settings.
  • This data stays on your device. You can delete it by clearing the App’s data or by uninstalling the App.

3. Account and cloud backup (optional)

  • If you sign in with Google, Apple or email, we use Firebase Authentication (by Google). It keeps your email address, a user ID and how you signed in. With Apple you can choose to hide your email address.
  • While you are signed in, your favourites, own words, flashcard progress, study history, lesson progress, AI corrections, main settings and profile name and picture are saved in Cloud Firestore (Google Cloud, Tokyo region), so they are the same on all your devices. Only your own account can read this data.
  • To delete your account: in the App, Menu → Account → Delete account. This deletes the account and its cloud data at once. You can also ask us by email (see “Contact”) and we will delete it.

4. AI features

  • AI Practice, AI Writing check and AI word explanations send the text needed for the answer (your messages or writing, the word, the scene and the level) to Google’s Gemini AI through Firebase AI Logic. Your name, email address and account ID are not sent with it.
  • Google processes this text under the Gemini API terms. On the free service, Google may use it to improve its products, and people at Google may review it. Do not enter personal or sensitive information into AI features.
  • The number of AI messages you used today is counted on your device.

5. App security (App Check)

To stop misuse of the AI and cloud services, Firebase App Check checks that requests come from the real App on a real device. It uses Google Play Integrity (Android) or Apple App Attest and DeviceCheck (iPhone), which send device and app integrity information to Google or Apple. It is not used to identify you.

6. Settings from our server (Remote Config)

The App downloads settings, such as the daily AI limit, with Firebase Remote Config. Firebase uses an app installation ID for this, not your name or account.

7. Ads (Google AdMob)

  • The free version shows a rewarded ad only when you tap a button such as “Watch a short ad” (for example, to get more AI messages). No ad is shown without your tap. Premium has no ads.
  • To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device’s advertising ID (Android), IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent with Google’s consent form. You can change your choice at any time: Menu → “Ad choices”.
  • On iPhone, the App does not ask to track you, so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used.

8. Purchases (Premium)

Premium is bought through Google Play or the Apple App Store. They handle the payment; we only learn whether Premium is active. We never see your card details.

9. Other features

  • Speaking practice uses the microphone and your phone’s speech recognition (by Google on Android, by Apple on iPhone), which may process your voice on their servers. The App does not save recordings or send them to us. The App asks for permission first.
  • Scan text uses the camera or a photo you choose. The text is read on your device, and the photo is not uploaded.
  • Pronunciation uses the speech engine on your device.
  • Daily reminders are notifications set on your device, only if you turn them on.
  • The App downloads dictionary corrections and learning content (such as listening audio). No personal information is sent with these downloads.
  • “Report a mistake” and “Send feedback” open your own email app. An email is sent only if you send it. It contains what you write and basic information about the App and your device, to help us fix problems.

10. Permissions

  • Internet: AI, sign-in, sync, ads and updates
  • Microphone and speech recognition: speaking practice (optional)
  • Camera and photos: Scan text (optional)
  • Notifications: daily reminder (optional)
  • Advertising ID (Android, added by Google Mobile Ads): ads

11. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Data is shared only with the service providers named above (Google and Apple), which process it to provide their services. Their servers may be outside your country.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Firebase privacy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Gemini API terms: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms

12. How long data is kept

  • Data on your device: until you delete it or the App.
  • Cloud data: until you delete your account or ask us to delete it.
  • Emails: only as long as needed to answer you and improve the App.

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete your data.

13. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

14. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and cloud data can be read only by its own account. No method is 100% secure, but we use reasonable measures to protect your data.

15. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version is shown here with a new date. If the changes are important, we will tell you in the App.

16. Contact
