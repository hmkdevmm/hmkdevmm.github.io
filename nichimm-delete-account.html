Delete your account and data

NichiMM (Japanese–Myanmar dictionary and JLPT study) — an app by HMK DEV MM

Signing in to NichiMM is optional. If you created an account, you can delete it and the data linked to it at any time.

Delete it in the app (immediately)

  1. Open NichiMM and sign in.
  2. Open the menu ☰ → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and the data saved with it in the cloud are deleted at once.

Or ask by e-mail

If you can no longer open the app, e-mail hmkdevjp@hotmail.com with NichiMM – delete my account in the subject line, from the e-mail address of your account (or tell us the address you signed in with). The account and its data are deleted within 30 days, and you get a reply when it is done.

What is deleted, what is kept

DataWhere it is heldWhen it is deleted
Account: e-mail address, name, sign-in ID (Google, Apple or e-mail)Firebase Authentication (Google)Immediately, with “Delete account”
Synced data: favourites, your own words, flashcard progress, lessons, AI corrections, main settingsCloud Firestore (Google)Immediately, with “Delete account”
Everything else you use in the app (history, downloads, AI chats, your profile photo)On your phone onlyWhen you uninstall the app or clear its data
Purchases (Premium)Google Play / App StoreKept by the store under its own rules; deleting your account does not cancel a subscription — cancel it in Google Play or the App Store
Advertising data (AdMob)GoogleReset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Privacy → Ads)

Text you sent to the AI is processed by Google's Gemini AI to make the reply and is not stored by NichiMM.
